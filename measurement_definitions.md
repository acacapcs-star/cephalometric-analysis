# Measurement definitions

All built from three primitives: **angle-at-point**, **angle-between-lines**,
**point-to-line distance**.

| Measurement | Landmarks | Formula | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| SNA | S, N, A | ∠ at N (S–N–A) | Maxilla position vs cranial base |
| SNB | S, N, B | ∠ at N (S–N–B) | Mandible position vs cranial base |
| ANB | SNA, SNB | SNA − SNB | Maxillo-mandibular difference (signed: keeps Class III) |
| Convexity | N, A, Pog | ∠ at A (N–A–Pog) | Facial convexity |
| Facial | Por-Or, N-Pog | angle between lines | Chin prominence |
| Y-axis | S-Gn, Por-Or | angle between lines | Growth direction |
| FMA | Por-Or, Go-Me | angle between lines | Mandibular plane to FH |
| FMIA | Por-Or, L1a-L1t | angle between lines | Lower incisor to FH |
| IMPA | Go-Me, L1a-L1t | 180 − angle | Lower incisor to mandibular plane (obtuse) |
| Interincisal | U1a-U1t, L1a-L1t | angle between lines | Angle between upper & lower incisors |
| U1-SN | S-N, U1a-U1t | 180 − angle | Upper incisor to anterior cranial base |
| U1-FH | Por-Or, U1a-U1t | 180 − angle | Upper incisor to FH |
| Gonial | Ar, Go, Me | ∠ at Go | Gonial angle |
| Ramus | Ar-Go, Por-Or | angle between lines | Ramus inclination |
| AB-plane | N-Pog, A-B | angle between lines | AB plane to facial plane |
| FH-SN | Por-Or, S-N | angle between lines | Angle between FH and SN |
| U1-APog | U1t, A-Pog | point-to-line × mm/px | Upper incisor to A–Pog line (mm) |
| U1-NPog | U1t, N-Pog | point-to-line × mm/px | Upper incisor to N–Pog line (mm) |

**Self-check:** FMA + FMIA + IMPA = 180° (geometric identity).
